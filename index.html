STRUXA BY HAND

One Revit tab. Twenty-plus productivity tools.

STRUXA combines Excel ↔ Revit data exchange, sheets and views, printing and exports, shop drawings, Auto Dimension, Auto Tag, model health, batch parameter editing and other productivity workflows.
