WHAT HAND RECALL DOES

Simple tools for real-world organization

HAND Recall is designed to keep everyday organization practical: capture where things are, group them logically, add photos and notes, set reminders, and retrieve information quickly.

01
Items & locations

Save belongings with notes, photos and location details so you can remember where they are.

02
Storage boxes

Group related items into storage boxes and keep household or work storage easier to manage.

03
Fast search

Search saved items, boxes, locations and recalls from one place.

04
Recalls & reminders

Create notes and reminders for information you want to remember later.

05
Local backup & restore

Export and restore application data using local backup files under your control.

06
Privacy-first design

HAND Apps does not operate a server that receives or stores your HAND Recall notes, items, boxes, locations or photos.

PRIVACY & GOOGLE SERVICES

Clear about what leaves your device

Your structured HAND Recall content is currently kept on-device. The app uses Google AdMob for advertising and Google Play Services for user-initiated Google authorization.

Advertising

Google AdMob may process device and advertising identifiers, IP-based approximate location, app or advertising interactions, and diagnostic information. Where applicable, Google UMP provides privacy choices.

Google Drive authorization

HAND Recall currently includes authorization groundwork for a future optional backup feature using the restricted drive.appdata scope. The current version does not upload your HAND Recall user-created content to Google Drive through this feature.
